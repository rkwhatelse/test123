Mandala Art Program

From teaching an art form to creating a marketable transformation.

The coach knew how to teach Mandala Art, but had no clear revenue model around it.

We identified an audience of women 30+ in South India interested in self-exploration, clarity and restarting their careers.

We repositioned Mandala Art around the transformation they were seeking and developed:

“Empower yourself and gain clarity through simple, guided doodling.”

We then built the audience, messaging and customer journey around that positioning.

The Shift

From teaching an art form
to selling a meaningful transformation.

AI Career Program

From a generic AI course to a career-focused offer.

“Learn AI” had become a crowded message.

We looked deeper into the problem and identified mid-career technology professionals struggling with repetitive work and the pressure to stay relevant.

We positioned AI as a career leverage skill, helping professionals reduce repetitive work, improve productivity and create more value.

The offer became:

“Stop spending your valuable time on routine work. Master AI to reduce repetitive work by up to 75% and increase your productivity.”

We then built the audience, messaging and funnel around professionals showing the strongest intent to move into the next-level program.

The Shift

From “Learn AI”
to “Use AI to create career leverage.”
